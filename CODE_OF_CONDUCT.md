# Code of conduct

Bilby welcomes participants of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful, focus criticism on the work, and make room for differing views.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publishing someone's
private information are not acceptable.

These standards apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other project discussions.

For moderation help that does not involve private information, mention
[@Evenss](https://github.com/Evenss) in the relevant discussion. Use GitHub's
**Report content** or **Report abuse** option for sensitive reports instead of
posting personal details publicly.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, lock discussions, or restrict
participation to address violations. Decisions should reflect the behavior and
its impact on the community.
